# Dynamic signals and headless scope lifetimes, 2026-10-27

Based on static-core checkpoint `645df95`. This completes the authorized bounded
headless topology/lifetime tranche, not the full Stage 1 roadmap.

## Implemented and reviewed

- Stable-equality flatMap, private between-pass topology updates, staged dynamic
  reads and rollback, selector failure/recovery, typed dependency-cycle and pass
  budget failure, and retirement of unrooted inner derivations.
- Public Scope cell/effect/child/dispose/onDispose behavior, plus headless scoped
  observations/reads, static positional mounts and generation-checked callbacks.
  Scope creation and disposal participate in transactions and refusal attempts.
  Disposal disconnects descendants, cancels queued effects and blocks publications.
- Owner/key/type identity through explicit owner/position/definition mounts.
  Definition replacement resets that position. Diagnostic child names are not
  reconciliation keys. No component renderer/reconciler is implemented here.
- Provisional snapshots refuse. First publication uses final staged initialization
  at revision zero; later unequal commits advance once. Leases registered during
  staging are compensated on rollback. Committed leases release once on disposal,
  independently reporting errors under read-only guards.

The design and independent review are recorded in
`../design/decisions/2026-10-27-topology-lifetimes.md`. Review found and corrected
late-attachment publication of disposed values, callbacks ignoring staged disposal,
and a missing mount/write revision case in the oracle. Final source-only re-review
found no remaining blocker in this bounded slice, conditional on the checks below.
The reviewer ran no builds. Main session ran all checks after the final changes.

## Limits and next work

Scope operations outside the supported subset raise `KernelError.Deferred`.
Events, deadlines, tasks/commands, channels, keyed component reconciliation,
Inputs, rendering, focus and native/GPU retirement remain later gates. Source
ownership remains live even when its data is not observed. Explicit runtime reads
pin roots until close; scoped reads retire their roots on disposal. Explicitly
pinned references can retain tombstone graph metadata without extending a disposed
cell's write capability or lease lifetime.

The driver still copies/traverses graph metadata; large-graph performance is
unqualified. Cycle errors identify a dependency cycle, while settle budget
exhaustion remains distinct; full Event/settle tracing is C1.3 work. The reference
models do not establish task/command policy, native ownership or full Stage 1 exit.

Next: C1.5b live Inputs and keyed headless reconciliation, then task/command
scheduling with expanded reference coverage. Full Stage 1 still needs all laws,
independent semantic review and the authoring corpus reviewed against the real API.
The pinned-upstream GC pause gate is unchanged and separate from these checks.
